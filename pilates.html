Pilates for a stronger foundation.

Pilates combines controlled movement, breathing and focus. Sessions can be adapted to experience and goals, with emphasis on quality of movement rather than rushing through repetitions.

  • Core strength and control
  • Posture and body awareness
  • Flexibility and mobility
  • Balanced, mindful movement
  • Guidance to help you progress at a comfortable pace

Suitability and exercise modifications depend on individual needs. Let the instructor know about any injury, pain or relevant health considerations before class.
